01 / About me

Hi, a little
more about me.

I'm originally from Pune, India, and now live in Belgium. My career has taken me into warehouses, planning environments and conversations with teams across different countries and time zones.

I enjoy the part where business needs meet technical reality: listening to the people using a system, understanding the details, and working out how to make the pieces fit together.

My work is technical, but the way I approach it is personal. Good solutions depend as much on clear communication and ownership as they do on the technology.
